# Ligarent asset sources

The product cutouts are AI-assisted background extractions from actual Caterpillar assets; they are not pixel-identical masks. Original manufacturer files are retained separately in the asset source folder. Exact model confidence high because image URLs appear as primary images on manufacturer model pages and model decals are visible. Views are three-quarter studio views, not strict side elevation. Technical drawing artwork is based on model-specific manufacturer diagrams. The final wireframe treatment is generated and decorative; refer to the original brochures for certified dimensions. Caterpillar material is copyright Caterpillar; no open reuse license identified.

## Machine card specifications
- D6R: Cat D6R brochure [AEHQ6149-02](https://catmachine.co.in/assets/pdf/D6R-Product-Brochure.pdf): ISO 9249 net power 130 kW (STD) or 145 kW (XL/LGP); SU blade 5.61 m³ and 3260 mm wide.
- D7R: Cat D7R brochure [AEHQ6414-01](https://brookshire.com.au/BrooksHire/media/Media/Spec%20Sheets/Cat-D7-Dozer.pdf): ISO 9249 rated net power 179 kW; STD/XR SU blade 6.86 m³ and 3693 mm wide.
- D8R: [Caterpillar D8R brochure](https://s7d2.scene7.com/is/content/Caterpillar/C785813): ISO 9249 rated net power 226 kW; standard SU blade 8.70 m³ and 3937 mm wide.

The power and SU blade figures are catalog values for the specified versions, not verified specifications for individual rental units. Confirm the exact configuration before quoting a job. Card weights remain the values in the supplied design and are labeled approximate.
